Privacy & ownership

Your data belongs to you

ZIVO is built as a private, personal application. The content you create — your moments, workouts, meals, expenses, and everything else — is yours, stored to power the app you use every day. Optional integrations, like Google Drive backup and Spotify, are entirely user-controlled: nothing leaves your account unless you choose it.

Google Drive backup

Optional Google Drive backup

ZIVO supports optionally backing up the media you create or select in the app to your own Google Drive.

  • Each ZIVO user connects their own Google account.
  • ZIVO does not use a shared or centralized Google Drive account.
  • Google Drive backup is entirely optional — nothing is connected by default.
  • ZIVO requests only the permissions needed for its backup functionality.
  • The integration is intended for backing up files ZIVO itself creates or manages.
