Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Overview

ZIVO is a private, personal application built to help one person organize the different parts of their daily life — moments, notes, tasks, schedule, expenses, university, and workouts — in one place. This policy explains what information ZIVO stores, how it is used, and how the optional Google Drive backup integration works.

ZIVO does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not use your content to train third-party models.

Information ZIVO Stores

ZIVO stores the information needed to run your account and the content you create in the app. This includes account/authentication details and the personal content you add across ZIVO's features.

Authentication & Account Information

ZIVO uses Firebase Authentication to sign you in, with Google, Apple, or email/password as sign-in options. Depending on the method you choose, ZIVO receives basic account details such as your name, email address, and a unique account identifier. This identifier is used to keep your data scoped to your account only.

User-Generated Content

Content you create in ZIVO — such as moments, notes, tasks, schedule entries, expenses, university items, and workout data — is stored in your account so the app can display it back to you across your devices. This content is private to your account and is not visible to other users.

Media & Local Storage

Where ZIVO features let you attach a photo (for example, in Moments), the app accesses your device's photo library only to let you pick or capture that image. Media you add is stored so it can be shown back to you within the app.

Firebase Services

ZIVO is built on Google Firebase, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (ZIVO's database), and Cloud Functions (used, for example, to power ZIVO's AI Assistant). Data in transit between your device and these services is encrypted, and Firestore security rules restrict your data so that only your authenticated account can read or write it.

Google Drive Integration

ZIVO includes an optional Google Drive backup feature. It is designed to use Google's drive.file scope — the most restrictive level of Drive access Google offers. This scope only allows ZIVO to see and manage the specific files that ZIVO itself creates, or that you explicitly open with ZIVO. It does not give ZIVO access to your existing files, folders, or any other content already in your Google Drive. Where this feature is not yet available in the version of ZIVO you are using, this section describes how it is designed to work once it is enabled.

What Happens When You Connect Google Drive

If you choose to enable Google Drive backup, you will sign in with your own Google account and grant ZIVO permission to create and manage files on your behalf, limited to the drive.file scope described above. ZIVO does not use a shared or centralized Google Drive account — each user's backup lives in that user's own Google Drive, under their own control.

What Permissions Are Requested

ZIVO requests only the permission needed to create, read, and manage the specific files it uses for backup. ZIVO does not request, and cannot use, broader access to the rest of your Google Drive.

How Google Drive Data Is Used

Files backed up through this feature are used solely to let you back up and restore ZIVO-created media across your devices. ZIVO does not scan, analyze, or share the contents of your Google Drive for any other purpose, and does not use this data for advertising.

Data Sharing

ZIVO does not sell or rent your personal data. ZIVO shares data only with the infrastructure providers necessary to operate the app — currently Google Firebase/Google Cloud (for authentication, storage, and app logic) and, only if you opt in, your own Google Drive.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your content is retained in your ZIVO account until you delete it or delete your account. If you connect Google Drive backup, the files ZIVO creates there remain in your own Drive until you delete them or disconnect the integration; ZIVO does not control retention of files once they exist in your Drive.

Your Control Over Your Data

You control the content you store in ZIVO and can remove it at any time within the app. Google Drive backup is opt-in and can be disconnected at any time, both from within ZIVO and from your Google Account's third-party access settings.

Security

ZIVO relies on Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules to ensure that only your authenticated account can access your data, and encrypts data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but ZIVO is built to keep your personal content scoped to you.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as ZIVO's features evolve, including as the Google Drive integration is completed. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the most recent revision.
